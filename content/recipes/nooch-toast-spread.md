# Nooch Toast Spread
Makes about 16oz of a breakfast spread full of omega 3s/6s, protein, fiber, and b12

## Soy Buttermilk
- ¾ cup unsweetened soy milk
- 1 tsp neutral oil (needed to emulsify best, can omit)
- 1 tsp apple cider vinegar
- microwave in a safe measuring cup for 30 seconds, let sit for a couple minutes

## Hempseed Mayo
- 1 cup hemp hearts
- 0 - ¼ cup water to thin if necessary
- turn on blender and slowly pour in the buttermilk until it starts to blend
- wait to add the rest of the buttermilk until it's running smooth
- should be pourable to reduce waste

## Cheesey Flavor
- 16+oz storage container of choice
- ½ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp msg
- ½ tsp sodium free curry powder (or tumeric + fenugreek powder if you have it)
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp sumac (or something else lemony/acidic)
- add a splash of water and mix well

## Combine
- blended hempseed mayo
- cheese spice liquid
- ½-¾ cup nutritional yeast, to taste and consistency, depending on how much water you added most likely
- looking for a hummus like consistency
- should taste good, but a little sharp, will become nutty and savory when toasted on bread
- will last in fridge for about a week

## Enjoy!
