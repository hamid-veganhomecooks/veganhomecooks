For about 6 to 9 "ladle" portions (120-ish ml each):

400 g silken tofu
160 g soaked cashews
40 g soy sauce or tamari
30 g - 40 g nooch
8 g kala namak, or to taste
500 ml water

Blend in food processor or w/ a stick blender to a fine cream.

For one portion, we usually 
- fried up a handful of some vegan "guanciale" which is either smoky seitan bits or smoked tofu in a pan w/ olive oil
- added a similar amount of onion
- when both were nicely fried we added a soup ladle full of the sauce, a splash of pasta water and the cooked pasta to the pan
- then we let it cook in the sauce for half a minute to reduce/incorporate the sauce
- then we plated it
