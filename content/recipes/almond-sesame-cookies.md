- 1/2 cup date paste
- 1/4 cup tahini
- 1 1/2 cups almond flour
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1/4 tsp ground cardamom
- pinch of salt
- 1/3 cup sesame seeds

1. Preheatt the oven to 350f. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper and set aside

2. Combine the date paste and tahini in a large bowl

3. In a separate large bowl, combine the almond flour, baking soda, cardamom and salt

4. Add the dry mixture to the wet mixture, mix until a dough is formed

5. Divide the dough into 12 equal portions and roll into balls

6. Pour the sesame seeds into a small bowl or onto a plate. Roll the balls in the sesame seeds to coat them completely and place them on the parchment-lined baking sheet

7. Using the bottom of a cup and a piece of parchment paper, flatten the balls to about 1/2 inch thick

8. Bake for 8 to 10 minutes, or until the sesame seeds are slightly golden
