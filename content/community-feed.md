Fresh posts straight from our [Lemmy community](https://lemmy.world/c/veganhomecooks) 
a show-and-tell hub where the vegans post what they're cooking.

[Lemmy](https://join-lemmy.org/) is a decentralized Reddit-style
discussion platform. You don't need an account to browse, just click any post
below to read it and its comments on Lemmy. You'll only need an account if you
want to comment or post yourself. You can make an account on any lemmy server to interact with the community like [lemmy.zip](https://lemmy.zip), [lemmy.ml](https://lemmy.ml), or [dbzer0](https://lemmy.dbzer0.com). Since is decentralized the community is available on most lemmy instances!

This page rebuilds about once a day, the [community page itself](https://lemmy.world/c/veganhomecooks)
is always current.
